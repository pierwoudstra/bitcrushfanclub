bitcrush fanclub <3 is a collective made of young multidisciplinary artists with a common interest in digital music and art. ‘fanclub’ means to us being fans of eachother; practicing fan culture symbolises our strive towards collectivity in art and life. bitcrush fanclub <3 consists of experimental pop artists and visual makers who are mostly found in inclusive spaces. we center fluid identity and socially accessible art.

12 June : PIXIECLUB @ Popradar Den Haag


Welcome to the digital, dark fairytale forest of PIXIECLUB. Be transported from the parallel world in your computer to Popradar to experience performances by Luid met jou, ms. palace, nano and in an uncanny visual world. Every fantasy world has a flip side full of shadow magic: How can bitcrush fanclub’s pixel fairies take up space in a system that was not built for them?
